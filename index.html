How to play

1

Pick a topic

Movies, foods, places, animals and more. Choose Everything for a mix, or Words to play with the whole English dictionary.

2

Pick a mountain

26 real peaks from 26 countries in four tiers, ranked by their real reputation. Beginner peaks (Fuji, Kilimanjaro) ask easy questions and go easy on rarity. Expert peaks (Aoraki, Everest, K2) ask only hard questions, judge rarity strictly and burn oxygen fast, but score ×2. Reach Levels 3, 6 and 10 to open the Intermediate, Advanced and Expert peaks. Height sets the length: Fuji is short, Kilimanjaro is epic.

3

Answer with something rare

Every prompt has many right answers. The less obvious yours is, the higher you climb. Here is “Name a pizza topping”:

4

Watch your oxygen

The O₂ bar drains all the time, and faster as you get higher. Right answers refill it, rare ones refill more. A wrong answer costs 4 seconds. Don't know a prompt? You get one free swap per ascent; after that a skip costs 6 seconds.

5

Chain rare answers

Two Glacier-or-rarer answers in a row start a combo. Each one after that adds ×0.25 to the height you gain, up to ×2. A common answer, a miss or a skip breaks it. Typos never break it: you get a spelling suggestion instead.

6

Summit, then keep going

Reach the top and your oxygen refills. Then the next ascent starts with thinner air. There is no last round, so the climb only ends when you run out of air.

7

Collect rare finds

First Ascent answers are the top rarity band, the kind about 1 player in 100 thinks of. The first one you hit on each prompt goes into your Rare Finds journal, so there is always another page to fill.

9

Level up

Every climb, summit and achievement earns XP. Levels unlock new topics, harder peaks, climber skins in the Locker and seasons that change the sky. Check Achievements for missions and the full reward list.

8

Climb the leaderboard

Post your height under a username to see where you rank. The Daily Expedition gives everyone the same mountain, three topics and prompts each day, with its own board. It resets at 1:00 AM Central with new topics and questions.

About Tampico

The idea

Most trivia games reward the first answer that pops into your head. Tampico does the opposite. Everyone can name pepperoni as a pizza topping; naming nduja gets you to the top. Each prompt has a long list of accepted answers ranked from the most obvious to the most obscure, and your rank decides how far you climb.

The name

Tampico is a port city on Mexico's Gulf coast, right at sea level. Every climb in the game starts from the bottom, so it felt like the right place to begin.

The mountains

All 26 peaks are real, one per country, and so are their routes. Base camps, huts and high camps use their real altitudes, from the 5th Station on Mount Fuji to the Hillary Step on Everest.

The answers

Topic prompts use hand-ranked lists. Word prompts check against a dictionary of about 400,000 English words, ranked by how often each word is actually used. The game also learns from you: answers you lean on drift toward the common end over time.
